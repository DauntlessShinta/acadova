How it works

Three steps, one ledger.

1

List what you can teach

Add your strong subjects to your profile so other students can find you when they search.

2

Request or accept a session

Ask a tutor for help, or accept a request from someone learning what you teach.

3

Complete it, rate it

When a session finishes, credits move automatically and both sides leave a rating.

About

Built for students who can't always afford a tutor.

Credits, not cash

Every account starts with a small credit balance. Teaching a session earns credits; learning spends them — nobody pays out of pocket.

One account, two roles

You're not locked into "tutor" or "learner." List what you can teach and what you want to learn, and use either side whenever it fits.

Accountability built in

Every session has a status you can track, and every completed session can be rated — so reputation carries real signal.
