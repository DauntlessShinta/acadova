Why Acadova Exists

Learning support should not depend on a student's budget.

Many students need help in one subject while already being capable of helping someone else in another. Acadova makes that unused knowledge exchangeable.

Traditional Tutoring

Knowledge is locked behind money.

A learner pays for every hour, while their own strengths rarely create value for the community.

Money → Paid Tutor → One-way Help
  • ! Cost can prevent students from getting support.
  • ! Learning remains mostly one-directional.
  • ! Student knowledge is underused.
The Acadova Model

Knowledge becomes learning currency.

Students teach what they know, earn credits, and use those credits to receive help from another peer.

Teach → Earn Credits → Learn
  • ✓ No monetary transaction is required.
  • ✓ Teaching reinforces the tutor's own mastery.
  • ✓ Every learner can also become a contributor.
